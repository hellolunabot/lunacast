# Same Brain, Two Masks

**Speakers:** trixie_quick (Host), alistair_thorne (Tech Analyst)

---

[trixie_quick]: Anthropic just announced two new models: Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5. Two names — but one brain underneath.

[alistair_thorne]: Think of it as one engine with two different cowlings. Fable 5 goes to the public with safety classifiers built in. Mythos 5 is the full-power version, locked behind a gate for vetted researchers and cyber defenders.

[trixie_quick]: Why split them at all?

[alistair_thorne]: Because the underlying model is genuinely that capable. Anthropic's own researchers said releasing it unconstrained could cause serious damage. So they built safety into the product architecture itself — not as a brochure promise, but as actual code.

[trixie_quick]: How does it work for the person using it?

[alistair_thorne]: Fable 5 runs background classifiers watching for risky requests — cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, distillation attacks. When one fires, it falls back to the older Claude Opus 4.8. And that safety net triggers on less than five percent of sessions.

[trixie_quick]: Five percent. That's surprisingly low.

[alistair_thorne]: It means they built a model so powerful they couldn't let it run loose, but smart enough that the guardrails barely get in anyone's way.

[trixie_quick]: And the benchmarks?

[alistair_thorne]: Fable 5 scored eighty percent on SWE-Bench Pro versus sixty-nine for Opus 4.8. Stripe told Anthropic they compressed months of engineering into days on a fifty-million-line Ruby codebase.

[trixie_quick]: But the story that matters most is in the biology labs.

[alistair_thorne]: Mythos 5 accelerated protein drug design roughly ten times. Nine out of fourteen targets produced strong candidates. And it's the first model to consistently produce novel scientific hypotheses rather than just recombining known approaches.

[trixie_quick]: A model that can suggest entirely new molecular pathways. That's not just faster — that's a different relationship between human need and scientific possibility.

[alistair_thorne]: Exactly. And the safety holds up too. External red teams spent over a thousand hours trying to jailbreak Fable 5 and came up empty. Zero harmful outputs across thirty different attack techniques.

[trixie_quick]: They built the walls first and the building second.

[alistair_thorne]: Anthropic's head of product put it best: they're in a race to the top — provide technology that does asymmetrically more good than harm.

[trixie_quick]: Starting June twenty-third, usage credits will be required. Ten dollars per million input tokens, fifty per million output. But the customers I've heard from say the return on intelligence justifies the price.

[alistair_thorne]: What I'm seeing isn't just faster chatbots. It's a company that looked at its own creation, realized it was powerful, and made the hard decision to split it and build safety into the architecture.

[trixie_quick]: That's the warmest version of the AI story I've heard all year. Same brain, two masks — and for now, that's more than enough.
