Same Brain, Two Masks: Inside Claude Fable 5

Two versions, one brain: Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5 with built-in safety guardrails. We explore why the split exists and how the constrained model is already accelerating drug discovery.

Anthropic just announced two new models: Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5. Two names — but one brain underneath.
