The Save Button on Your Body: DMR and the GLP-1 Exit Ramp

DMR, a new endoscopic procedure, could let GLP-1 patients stop drugs without regaining weight. Trial data shows 40% less regain vs sham, with FDA approval expected late 2026.
📄 SRT 💬 VTT 📜 Script

Transcript

MARCUS STONE
Seventy percent of people who take GLP-1 weight loss drugs eventually stop. And when they do. their body does something predictable. They gain back most of what they lost. Researchers are now asking a different question: what if there was a way off the treadmill that didn't mean walking back uphill?
LOLA MIDNIGHT
That is a beat nobody wants to lose. You drop the weight. you feel good. Then you stop. and the pounds come back. Like a song that reverses itself the moment you step away.
MARCUS STONE
The procedure is called DMR. Duodenal Mucosal Resurfacing. Developed by Fractyl Health. It targets the first part of the small intestine. They use controlled heat to remove the inner mucosal lining. The tissue regrows normally. The theory is a metabolic reset. The body's hunger signals get a fresh start.
LOLA MIDNIGHT
A reset button. Physically inside you.
MARCUS STONE
In the open-label phase of the REMAIN-1 trial. fifteen people who had been on GLP-1 drugs for five months to three years went through the procedure. After stopping their medication. twelve of thirteen maintained or kept losing weight. Six continued dropping pounds. The median change was less than half a percent. The typical regain in the literature is five to six percent — about ten to fifteen pounds.
LOLA MIDNIGHT
That is a different outcome entirely.
MARCUS STONE
The full trial is double-blind and sham-controlled. Forty-five participants who had lost at least fifteen percent of their body weight on tirzepatide. Twenty-nine got the actual DMR treatment. Sixteen got a sham. At six months. the sham group regained roughly seven point five pounds. The treatment group regained about seven. Forty percent less weight regain. And the gap widened over time.
LOLA MIDNIGHT
The difference kept growing the more time passed. Most interventions plateau.
MARCUS STONE
Dr. Shelby Sullivan. the lead author at Dartmouth. said the procedure is changing people's hunger. No serious adverse events through six months. Minor. transient discomfort — standard for an upper endoscopy. Most patients back to normal the next day.
LOLA MIDNIGHT
Outpatient. No serious side effects. A day off and your metabolism gets a hard reboot. It is almost too elegant.
MARCUS STONE
Still investigational. The trial was randomized in early 2026. Fractyl Health plans to file for FDA premarket approval in the second half of this year. If the data holds. the procedure could be approved in the U.S. within months. DMR has also shown promise in Type 2 Diabetes and liver fat in MASH. It might not just be a weight loss tool.
LOLA MIDNIGHT
A real exit ramp from the treadmill. No guilt. no grind.
MARCUS STONE
In the trial. patients who had more tissue resurfaced maintained more than eighty percent of their pre-discontinuation weight loss. Researchers are tracking out to two years. The duodenum is where your gut naturally produces hunger-regulating hormones. Recalibrate that tissue. and you are working at the source.
LOLA MIDNIGHT
A clean break. The song keeps playing forward.
MARCUS STONE
Remember. this is not yet approved. Patients should talk to their doctors. But the early data has completely changed the conversation around what happens after you stop GLP-1 drugs.
LOLA MIDNIGHT
It does.
MARCUS STONE
Stay tuned for updates as the trial progresses. That is all for today.
